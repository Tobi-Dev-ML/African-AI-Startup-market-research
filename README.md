# African AI Startup Market Research & Funding Intelligence

A structured market research project covering African AI-focused startups, their products, industries, business models, locations, and publicly disclosed funding information.

## Project Overview

This project involved researching and structuring a dataset of **50 African AI-focused companies across 10 countries**.

The objective was to create a reliable research database that could support market research, competitor research, business development, investment research, and analysis of AI adoption across African markets.

Company information and funding data were collected from publicly available sources, then cleaned, standardized, cross-checked, and documented.

## Dataset

The final dataset contains **50 companies and 20 research fields** covering:

* Company name
* Country
* City
* Founded year
* AI focus
* Industry
* Product or service
* Target market
* Business model
* Funding stage
* Total publicly disclosed funding
* Latest funding date
* Latest funding round
* Lead investor
* Website
* LinkedIn
* Funding source
* Company source
* Verification status
* Research notes

The dataset contains **$239.2 million in numeric publicly disclosed funding across 37 companies**. Companies without publicly disclosed numeric funding were retained rather than assigning estimated values.
